[ 11/6/26 ]
- The song library is now built into the mod and can be browsed from the in-game song list.
- Added a search bar. The gear button next to it opens a "Hide Vanilla Songs" toggle and a "Reload Songs" button.
- Your songs are now individual .txt files in Zomboid/Lua/BardToTheBoneSongLibrary/. Songs from BardToTheBone_Songs.txt are copied there once; the old file is left as a backup.
- Editing a mod (blue) or dropped-in .abc (green) song makes your own (white) copy. Only your songs can be saved or removed; Save and Remove grey out otherwise.
- Removing a song blanks its file (the game can not delete files). A new song with the same name reuses it.
- The editor can be collapsed with the button at the start of the button row. It starts collapsed and remembers your choice.
- "Load All" is now "Revert": it discards unsaved changes to the selected song (an unsaved copy goes back to its original).
- Fixed songs being cut short when the game ran below ~48 FPS.
- Fixed boredom/unhappiness reduction from nearby music not applying in multiplayer.
- Multiplayer: other players now hear your volume setting and each note's fade-out.
- Multiplayer: notes are now sent in small batches instead of one network message per note.
- Hide Vanilla Songs is now remembered in Zomboid/Lua/BardToTheBone_Settings.txt.
- Fixed the window briefly opening and closing when playing an instrument from a bag or the floor.
- Removed unused accordion .mid files.
- Song list, buttons and labels now follow vanilla UI styling and translations.

[ 11/8/26 ]
Part 1:
- Tweaked song playback.
-- Chord lengths are no longer ignored: the shortest note in a chord sets when the next note starts, and longer notes keep ringing.
-- Tied notes are held instead of played again. Long notes are no longer cut short.
-- Fixed accidentals carrying past barlines, missing x and Z rests, tuplets dropping their first note, and +p+ style markings being played as notes.
-- Tempo changes partway through a song no longer jump backwards.
-- Files with several parts (X:1, X:2) play the parts together, and each part can be picked from the voice button.
-- Silence before the first note is skipped.

Part 2:
- Song library cleaned up: every song is now named "Artist - Title (Part)", with matching T: and C: lines inside the file.
-- Your own songs show their C: artist in the list too.
- Removed 109 duplicate songs, keeping the most complete version of each.
- Instrument parts of the same arrangement are combined into one song.
-- The voice button can pick a part to play.

[ 11/9/26 ]
- New Performance Skills: Strings, Keys, Winds and Percussion.
 -- Playing an instrument earns XP in its family's skill.
 -- Busier songs earn more: note density, pitch range, chords, accidentals and rhythm variety all count.
 -- Replaying a song you know gives a small bonus (up to +20%)
 -- Playing new instruments gives +25% for its first 15 minutes.

- Each song gets a difficulty from 0 to 10 based on its notes.
 -- Low skill means sloppy playing.
 -- Mistakes shrink each level and stop at level 10.
 -- The song list shows each song's difficulty for the instrument in your hands (drums are rated separately).
 -- Long song titles are shortened with ... so the difficulty always fits.

- New sandbox page Bard to the Bone:
  - Attract Zombies (on by default).
  - Performance Skills (on by default). Turn off for no XP, no mistakes and no difficulty ratings.
  - XP Multiplier for Performance.

- Opening a door (or climbing through a window or over a fence) while playing now pauses the song and resumes it afterwards, instead of going silent.
